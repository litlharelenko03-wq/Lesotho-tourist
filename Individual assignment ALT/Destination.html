What to be aware of before your trip

Lesotho is a year-round destination! October to April is warm and great for hiking and horseback riding, while June to August offers snowy conditions perfect for skiing at Afriski.

While main highways connecting Maseru, Leribe, and Mohale's Hoek are paved, a 4x4 is strongly recommended when tackling Sani Pass or navigating remote mountain passes.
