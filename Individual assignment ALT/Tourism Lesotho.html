Reasons Visit Lesotho

The prestigious Mountain Kingdom of Lesotho is home to glorious mointain ranges, bottomless water reserves,rich culture and traditions, and boasts an impressive historical background. Lesotho is a country that beckons tourists from all corners of the world and although it is a relatively small country, the wonders to be experianced and the company to be enjoyed are endless in variety.

Bear witness to the iconic Mokhoro, traditional woven blankets-the Seanamrena, historic cave dwellings at Kome caves, and warm Basotho hospitality.
